FAQ

The questions we hear most

Are these the original designer perfumes?

No. Pure Scents blends are original formulas inspired by designer scents. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by any designer brand. The brand references help you find a familiar scent profile. The bottle you receive is made by Pure Scents.

How long does a scent last?

Most eau de parfum blends run four to eight hours on skin. Oils and roll-ons sit closer and can last longer or shorter depending on your skin. Heat, dry skin and application point all change the result. Ask for a tester in store before committing to a full bottle.

What is the difference between the sizes?

The 10ml roll-on is the trial size, the 30ml is the travel spray, and the 60ml is the full bottle. Price per ml drops as the bottle gets larger; every product card shows the exact figure.

Can I return a bottle I opened?

No. Opened fragrance cannot be resold, so opened bottles are final sale. If the scent is not right, come in and we will help you find a better match, and you can sample before buying again.

Do you ship outside the United States?

Not yet. The current shipping zone is the US only.

How do Mixology deposits work?

The deposit holds your time and comes off the session total. Reschedule more than 24 hours ahead and it moves with you. A no-show loses the deposit.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards cover any Mixology tier or any product, never expire, and can be used in store or online.
