How it works

Three steps, one hour

02

Adjust sweetness, smoke, freshness and strength. Your guide keeps the formula wearable and keeps the session moving.

Bottle it

We bottle and label your blend before you leave. We keep the formula so you can reorder it later.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need any experience?

No. The guide leads the session and narrows the library for you. Most people have a wearable blend within twenty minutes.

Can I bring someone?

Yes. A guest can watch at no charge or blend a second bottle for the price of the take-home product. Tell us in the notes so we set up the table.

What if I do not like the result?

You adjust during the session, not after. The guide will not bottle something you are unsure about. If the final blend is not right, we keep working within the session time.

How does the deposit work?

The deposit holds your time and comes off the session total. Reschedule up to 24 hours ahead. No-shows lose the deposit.

Can I reorder my blend later?

Yes. We keep the formula under your name, so a reorder is a bottle, not another session.

Can you ship my blend?

Yes, anywhere in the US. Local pickup is free at either store.
